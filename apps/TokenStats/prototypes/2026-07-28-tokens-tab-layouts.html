Wayfinder #27 · throwaway prototype · round 3

D3 picked — now paying down its 92pt

D3 was chosen: every figure kept, plus an explicit bar under each row. It came in at 648pt at 30 days, 92pt over the plain table and well past the 600pt comfort line. These two tightenings try to pay that back without giving up anything D3 was chosen for. The key move: the bar's segments run in the same order as the columns above them — in, out, cache write, cache read — so the table header already is the legend, and the legend can go.
