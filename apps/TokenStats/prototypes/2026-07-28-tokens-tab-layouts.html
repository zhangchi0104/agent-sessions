Naming the colours: header, legend, or both

D3 is settled — every figure, plus a bar beneath each row, at 528pt for 30 days. What is left is how the four colours get named. The abbreviated header (C·W) is terse and free; a legend spells Cache write out in full beside its swatch and costs 25pt, once. These three are the same layout differing only in that.
